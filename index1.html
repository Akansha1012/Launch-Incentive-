The rules of the beach

60 · 80 · 100

Cross 60% of your monthly target and you earn at 1x. 80% doubles it to 2x. 100% triples it to 3x. Below 60%, the month pays nothing.

2x CTC

No one can earn more than 2x their quarterly CTC (annual CTC ÷ 4) in a quarter. The streak bonus sits outside this cap.

Streak

Hit 100%+ for 3 months in a row and earn a 25% bonus. 6 months gets 50%, 12 months gets 100%.

SFDC

A deal counts only when its Sales Checklist is marked Done in SFDC. No entry, no incentive.

  • Each month is worked out on its own: amount booked × payout % × that month's multiplier. The three months are added, then the quarterly cap applies.
  • Multipliers move in steps: 60–79% = 1x, 80–99% = 2x, 100% and above = 3x. The deck does not show anything above 3x.
  • Streak bonus here is 25% of your capped quarterly payout, for a role that hits 100% in all three months. Check the exact base with MSS.
  • "Before" uses the old rates with the old multipliers (0.9x at 80%, 1.2x at 100%, 1.5x at 120%). KAM SM, Digital Campaign Manager and the two Heads have no target in the deck, so the target shown for them is an example. Overwrite it with yours.
  • CTC defaults follow the deck's illustration: ₹25 L for SMs, ₹35 L for TLs. The heads' ₹60 L is an example.
